Draft for reviewNov 3, 2026

Operating model

  • RA AT&T
    AT&T sets the business rules

    AT&T owns its customers, products, money and operations, and the source systems behind them. The marketplace consumes AT&T's answers and never recalculates them.

  • RA VTEX
    VTEX runs the platform

    VTEX operates the commerce platform and keeps the marketplace records, routes every order through the required checks and applies each result.

  • R Clouda A VTEX
    Clouda builds and maintains, under VTEX

    Clouda configures the platform, builds the storefront and every integration (including with third parties and AT&T's backend), and keeps them running. VTEX approves designs and certifies connectors.

  • RA Provider
    Providers decide within their service

    Each third party owns the decisions and follow-up inside its own service, under AT&T's contract and the rules AT&T approves.
